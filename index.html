The model is the easy half. Getting it to run somewhere real is the work.

I studied Computer Engineering at Misr Higher Institute for Engineering and Technology and graduated in 2026 with honours, finishing my final year at 94.66%. Graduation project

AI-powered plant disease detection

Completed 27 June 2026 6 months YOLOv8s · PyTorch · FastAPI · Raspberry Pi 5

View Hugging Face Space ↗

The problem

Diagnosing a crop disease correctly means knowing what hundreds of them look like, and the people who do are rarely standing in the field at the moment it matters. Anything useful has to cover many diseases across many crops, run fast enough to be used while walking a row, and keep working where there is no network.

What I built

  • An object detection system covering 44 disease classes across 12 crop species, trained on YOLOv8s.
  • Custom dataset-balancing and object-level copy-paste augmentation pipelines in Python with Albumentations, iterated until class imbalance stopped skewing the detector.
  • A cloud inference service built with FastAPI on Hugging Face Spaces, returning annotated result image URLs.
  • Real-time edge inference on a Raspberry Pi 5 so the same model runs offline, in the field.
  • A companion Flutter mobile interface prototype showing multi-detection results with severity and treatment recommendations.

Where it ended up

Research derived from the project — Intelligent Agricultural Robot for Precision and Sustainable Farming Using AI and IoT — was accepted for publication in IEEE Xplore and for presentation at ITC 2026. The project received support from ITIDA under its graduation projects support programme.
